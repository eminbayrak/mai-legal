Frequently Asked Questions

How does the subscription work?

Mai offers three tiers:

  • Free Trial (7 Days): Full access to all features
  • Free Tier (After Trial): Limited daily usage (5 chats, no podcasts)
  • Mai Pro ($4.99/mo): Generous limits (20 chats, 3 podcasts/day)

Why isn't my podcast generating?

Check the following:

  • Verify you haven't reached your daily limit (Free tier cannot generate podcasts)
  • Make sure your input text is at least 100 words
  • Check your internet connection
  • Upgrade to Mai Pro if you're on the Free tier

How do I reset my trial limits?

Trial limits reset automatically every 24 hours. You can check when your limits reset in Profile → Trial Usage.

Upgrade to Mai Pro for generous daily allowances.

Is my data private?

Yes! All your study data is stored locally on your device. We never send your personal data to our servers. AI content is processed through Google's secure Gemini API.

Can I export my data?

Data export is coming in a future update. For now, you can manually copy content from generated podcasts, flashcards, and study guides.

What's the difference between free and Pro?

Free Trial (7 Days):
• Full access to all features

Free Tier (After Trial):
• 5 chats/day
• 3 flashcard generations/day
• No podcasts or Join sessions

Mai Pro ($4.99/mo):
• 20 chats/day
• 3 podcasts/day
• 3 weekly Join sessions
• Priority support
