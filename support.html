Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create flashcards?

Tap the "+" button, upload a document (PDF, photo, or paste text), and Mai will automatically generate flashcards from your content.

How does the Screen Time feature work?

First, authorize Screen Time access in the app. Then select apps you want to block. Complete study sessions (flashcards, quizzes, podcasts) to earn unlock time for those apps.

Why isn't speech recognition working?

Make sure you've granted microphone permission in Settings > Mai. Speech recognition also requires an internet connection for non-English languages.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone to modify or cancel.

How do I delete my data?

All your data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will delete all your data. You can also reset data in the app's Settings.

The app blocked my apps but I can't earn unlock time. What do I do?

You need to complete a study session (flashcards, quiz, or podcast) to earn unlock time. If you're stuck, go to Settings > Screen Time > Mai and disable the extension temporarily.
