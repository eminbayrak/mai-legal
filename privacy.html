Privacy Policy

Mai - Daily Study Tracker & AI Tools

← Back to Home

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Mai collects minimal information:

  • Study Progress: Your study sessions, time spent, and streak data are stored locally on your device
  • Preferences: App settings like language, daily goals, and learning mode
  • User-Provided API Keys: If you're a free user, your Google Gemini or OpenAI API keys are stored securely in your device's keychain
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable information

2. How We Use Your Information

All data is stored locally on your device and is used to:

  • Track your study progress and streaks
  • Generate personalized AI content using your API keys
  • Sync your preferences across sessions
  • Provide usage statistics

We never send your data to our servers.

3. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All your study data, notes, and generated content are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage
  • No Cloud Backup: We do not automatically back up your data to the cloud
  • iCloud Sync: Future versions may support optional iCloud sync for your data

4. Third-Party Services

Mai uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini API: For AI-powered content generation (if you provide your API key)
  • OpenAI API: Alternative AI provider (if you provide your API key)

Your interactions with these services are subject to their privacy policies. We only send the content you explicitly choose to generate (e.g., your notes for creating a podcast).

5. Data Security

  • API keys are stored in iOS Keychain, Apple's secure credential storage system
  • All data remains on your device
  • No data is transmitted to our servers
  • We do not have access to your API keys or study content

6. Children's Privacy

Mai is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All data processing happens locally on the device.

7. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your study data within the app
  • Delete: Clear all app data through iOS Settings
  • Export: Future versions will support data export
  • Modify: Edit or delete any generated content

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the app.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at maiiosx.app@gmail.com.
