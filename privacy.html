Introduction

Mai ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Mai collects the following types of information:

  • User Profile: Name and age (stored locally on your device only)
  • Study Content: Documents, PDFs, and text you upload for learning (processed to generate flashcards and podcasts)
  • Usage Data: Study session duration and app usage statistics (stored locally)
  • Voice Data: Audio recordings during speaking quizzes (processed in real-time, not stored)

How We Use Your Information

  • Generate AI-powered flashcards and podcasts from your content
  • Validate spoken answers in quizzes using speech recognition
  • Track study progress and manage Screen Time rewards
  • Improve app functionality and user experience

Third-Party Services

Mai uses the following third-party services to provide AI features:

  • Google Gemini API: For content generation and AI processing
  • Apple Speech Recognition: For voice input processing

These services process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Most of your data is stored locally on your device. Content sent to AI services for processing is not permanently stored on external servers.

Screen Time & FamilyControls

Mai uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (FamilyControls) to help you manage app usage. This feature:

  • Allows you to block distracting apps until study goals are met
  • Requires your explicit authorization
  • Data stays on your device and with Apple's secure systems

Children's Privacy

Mai is designed to be safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is necessary for app functionality.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your local data at any time by uninstalling the app
  • Revoke microphone and Screen Time permissions in Settings
  • Contact us with questions about your data

Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us at:

Email: support@maiapp.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
